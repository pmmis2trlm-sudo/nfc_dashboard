🌾 NFC (NON-FARM COLLECTIVE)

A group of 15–20 women SHG members engaged in similar non-farm livelihood activities who come together to grow stronger, earn more and build a better future.
🔧 IMPROVE PRODUCTION Share skills, knowledge and experience to improve quality.
📦 REDUCE COSTS Buy raw materials in bulk to get lower costs.
📈 ACCESS BETTER MARKETING Sell together, reach more customers.
🛠️ COMMON FACILITIES Shared tools, machines and workspace.
✨ SKILL DEVELOPMENT Regular training to upgrade skills.
💰 FINANCIAL LINKAGES Credit, savings, insurance.
🤝 STRONGER TOGETHER Unity, trust and teamwork.
🎯 MAIN OBJECTIVE Increase members’ income through collective business.
💪 TOGETHER WE EARN MORE! Higher Income · Better Livelihoods · Women Empowerment · Stronger Communities · Sustainable Future
